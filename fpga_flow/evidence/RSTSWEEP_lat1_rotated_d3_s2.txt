  A pulse of ONE cycle is enough: the stretcher turns any level into a full
  LEN-cycle reset, so a bouncing button or a 1-cycle PS write cannot produce
  a short reset.  pulse=0 (no external reset at all) is deliberately NOT in
  this sweep: on silicon the core still resets (snowflake_reset_sync's counter
  powers up at 0, see the header), but the AXI register file would need a bus
  reset to leave X in RTL -- and on a real board the PS drives pl_resetn
  during configuration anyway, so the case cannot occur.
